Support

I developed Pace to help students return to schoolwork without requiring an explanation of why they fell behind. Add your tasks, check in with your capacity, and receive a plan that fits what you can manage today.

Getting help

Contact me at sohanm4364@gmail.com and describe the issue. Screenshots can help me understand it. I will respond as soon as possible, usually within a few days.

Installing Pace

  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): open planwithpace.app, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen."
  • Android (Chrome): open the same address and choose "Install app" from the menu (or the install prompt).
  • Computer: it works in any modern browser; Chrome and Edge also offer an install icon in the address bar.

Once loaded, Pace works fully offline.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I back up or move to a new device? Settings → Export gives you a JSON file. On the new device, Settings → Import merges it back in. Nothing is deleted by an import.

I cleared my browser data and my tasks are gone. Because Pace stores everything locally, clearing site data removes it. An export file is the only backup, so export once in a while.

Is Pace medical software? No. Pace is a planning tool. It is not a medical tool and does not provide medical advice.
